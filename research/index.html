Research

The evidence behind the work

Every brand plan on this site cites published research for its main choices. These are the 30 findings I drew on, with links to each source.

How I used them: I start from the audience and the brand's situation, pick the platform and format, then check each choice against data before writing a single post. A few caveats I keep in mind: some studies cover the UAE and Saudi Arabia together, some samples are US or global, a couple of sources are several years old, and DataReportal's platform figures measure ad reach, not active users.
