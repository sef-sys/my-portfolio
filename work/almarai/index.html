Almarai

Almarai already owns the family table; short form leftover recipes can carry its anti waste message daily.

This concept turns Almarai's Ramadan food waste message into a daily short form recipe habit. Families cook a second meal from what is left, using Almarai dairy and ALYOUM chicken, and post it back. It opens a TikTok recipe lane and gives families a reason to share their own plates.

04 Campaign

The big idea

Ramadan 2027, when iftar tables are at their fullest. It picks up the food waste theme from the 2026 musical 'Appreciate the Blessings'.

The Second Plate

الصحن الثاني

Mama Sarah & Sarah return with a Ramadan short form series where every episode starts from last night's iftar leftovers. Each recipe uses Almarai dairy or ALYOUM chicken to turn what is left into a full new dish.

How it spreads

Families post their own second plate on TikTok or Instagram with #الصحن_الثاني and tag @almarai. Each week Mama Sarah cooks one family's idea on air and credits their account.

  • Hook: open on the full fridge shelf vs open on the finished dish. Compare 3 second hold rate.
  • Voice: Mama Sarah VO vs on screen text with music only. Compare saves per 1,000 views.
  • Call to action: 'Post your second plate' vs 'Save this for tomorrow'. Compare hashtag entries against saves.
07 Research

Why it should work

Each major choice in this plan, matched to published research. Some studies cover the wider region or global audiences; the source says which. Full list on the research page.

Second Plate Recipes pillar (40% of content) on using iftar leftovers
Research cited by the UN shows that 25 to 50 per cent of food prepared during Ramadan in West Asia is wasted.

If a quarter to half of Ramadan food is wasted, a recipe that turns leftovers into a second meal solves a problem almost every family table has.

United Nations in Saudi Arabia, Fasting, Feasting and Food Waste in West Asia, 2021
The Second Plate campaign timed for Ramadan 2027
77% of adults surveyed in the UAE and Saudi Arabia say they cook more at home during Ramadan, and 59% buy groceries in bulk.

Most families cook more at home in Ramadan, so that is when Almarai products and leftover recipes meet the most kitchens.

Ipsos, TikTok Ramadan and Eid Study (1,600 adults in UAE and KSA), 2026
TikTok at 5 posts per week as the main recipe channel
7 in 10 people in the UAE and Saudi Arabia actively seek Ramadan related content across digital platforms, and TikTok users are 1.2 times more likely to discover brands they had not considered before.

People actively look for Ramadan content, so a daily Almarai recipe has demand waiting for it instead of interrupting a feed.

Ipsos, TikTok Ramadan and Eid Study (1,600 adults in UAE and KSA), 2026
Micro local home cooks who post budget family meals
In a 2026 survey of brands, 52.83% plan to expand work with micro creators and 51.43% with nano creators, against 20.59% for macro creators.

Brands are shifting budget toward micro creators, and a home cook with a real family is the right voice for a leftovers idea.

Influencer Marketing Hub, Influencer Marketing Benchmark Report 2026, 2026
Before spending the budget, I'd test this

Will mothers save and recreate leftover recipes from the Second Plate series more than standard Almarai recipe content during Ramadan?

Method

3 week A/B test on TikTok and Instagram Reels during Ramadan: Second Plate leftover recipes versus standard new recipes

Sample

12 recipe videos (6 per variant), plus a 150 person Instagram story poll of followers on iftar leftovers

What would confirm it

Second Plate videos reach a save rate at least 25% higher, and at least 60% of poll respondents say they cook too much at iftar
