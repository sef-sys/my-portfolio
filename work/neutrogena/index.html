Neutrogena

Turn Beauty to a Science into an Arabic dermatologist series built around Ramadan hydration.

Neutrogena already brought dermatologists to Gulf media with Beauty to a Science. This concept moves that trust into weekly Arabic short form, where viewers ask real skin questions and dermatologists answer. It peaks in Ramadan with a Hydro Boost routine series made for the hours between iftar and suhoor.

04 Campaign

The big idea

Ramadan 2027 (around February to March). Fasting days change sleep and routines, and viewers spend more time on their phones after iftar.

Science After Iftar

علم بعد الفطور

Every night of Ramadan, a local dermatologist answers one follower skin question in under a minute. Hydro Boost Hydrating Fluid SPF 50 anchors the morning step, and a simple hydration routine anchors the night.

How it spreads

Followers drop questions under the pinned post or in Story question boxes with #علم_بعد_الفطور; the best question each day gets a video reply. Creators stitch the answers with their own routines.

07 Research

Why it should work

Each major choice in this plan, matched to published research. Some studies cover the wider region or global audiences; the source says which. Full list on the research page.

Derm Explains pillar (40%) with dermatologists answering one follower question each
43% of young people aged 13 to 29 name dermatologists as their most trusted skincare source versus 9% for social media, yet 62% still turn to social media for advice and 53% feel confused about which products suit them.

Young people trust dermatologists most but still get skincare advice from social media, so putting dermatologists on social closes that gap.

Curology, The Clarity Gap (1,930 US respondents), 2026
Science After Iftar campaign during Ramadan 2027
7 in 10 people in the UAE and Saudi Arabia actively seek Ramadan related content across digital platforms, and TikTok users are 1.2 times more likely to discover brands they had not considered before.

Viewers actively look for Ramadan content, so routines for fasting days answer a question people are already asking.

Ipsos, TikTok Ramadan and Eid Study (1,600 adults in UAE and KSA), 2026
Snapchat daily during Ramadan for 18 to 30 year olds
Snapchat reaches over 90% of people aged 13 to 34 in Saudi Arabia, and users open the app over 50 times a day on average.

Snapchat reaches over 90% of young people in Saudi Arabia, who open it dozens of times a day, which suits daily routine reminders.

Arab News, If it's happening in the Kingdom, chances are it's on Snapchat, 2024
Micro ingredient focused skincare reviewers in Riyadh and Jeddah
In a 2026 survey of brands, 52.83% plan to expand work with micro creators and 51.43% with nano creators, against 20.59% for macro creators.

Brands are moving budget to micro creators, and ingredient reviewers speak to people who screenshot ingredient lists before buying.

Influencer Marketing Hub, Influencer Marketing Benchmark Report 2026, 2026
Before spending the budget, I'd test this

Do skincare viewers trust and save an answer more when an Arabic speaking dermatologist gives it on camera than when a creator gives the same answer?

Method

2 week A/B test on TikTok and Instagram Reels during Ramadan: Derm Explains with a dermatologist on camera versus the same script by a skincare creator

Sample

10 videos (5 per variant), plus a 120 person story poll on trust

What would confirm it

Dermatologist videos earn a save rate at least 30% higher, and at least 60% of poll respondents rate them more trustworthy
