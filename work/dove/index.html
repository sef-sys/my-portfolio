04 Campaign

The big idea

Launch in March 2027 around Mother's Day on 21 March, after Eid al Fitr, four years after #WelcomeToWomanhood first ran in Saudi Arabia.

Confidence Starts at Home

الثقة تبدأ من البيت

Saudi mothers sit down and answer the question they wish someone had answered for them at 12, using one page of the Dove Confidence Kit per episode. Daughters take part through drawings and voice notes, so the series stays private, warm and modest.

How it spreads

Each episode ends with a question for families to try at home, and parents share their own answer or their daughter's drawing with #الثقة_تبدأ_من_البيت. The link in bio opens the Confidence Kit.

07 Research

Why it should work

Each major choice in this plan, matched to published research. Some studies cover the wider region or global audiences; the source says which. Full list on the research page.

Mother and Daughter Talks pillar (40%) with a question to try at home
1 in 2 girls say idealized beauty content on social media causes low self esteem, and 80% of girls want their parents to talk with them about it.

Half of girls feel worse after idealized beauty content and most want their parents to talk with them, which is the exact gap this series fills.

Practical Dermatology, reporting Dove Self Esteem Project research (Edelman DXI, US), 2022
Snapchat daily Stories for mothers watching at night
71% of parents in Saudi Arabia are on Snapchat, according to a study by Kantar and Snap.

Most Saudi parents are on Snapchat, so daily Stories reach mothers in the app they already check after the kids sleep.

Communicate Online, Saudi's Snapchat Community Hits 20 Million Milestone (Kantar and Snap study), 2022
Real Saudi Women pillar showing local mothers and daughters
80% of people trust the brands they use, and 73% say their trust in a brand would increase if it authentically reflected today's culture.

Showing real Saudi families reflects the culture viewers live in, which this finding links to stronger trust in a brand.

Edelman, 2025 Edelman Trust Barometer Special Report: Brand Trust, From We to Me, 2025
The Research pillar and the Confidence Kit link in bio
81% of Saudi adults prefer to engage with brands online, and 66% say they will not buy from a brand that is not socially responsible, even if they like it.

Saudi consumers expect brands to act on social issues, and sharing Dove's self esteem research gives that commitment proof.

Ipsos, Spotlight KSA: Views on Brand Engagement (500 adults, Saudi Arabia), 2025
Before spending the budget, I'd test this

Do Saudi mothers act on a guided conversation prompt at home after watching a Mother and Daughter Talks episode?

Method

120 person Instagram story poll plus a follow up survey of mothers who watched a pilot episode

Sample

120 Saudi mothers aged 30 to 45 recruited from Dove KSA story viewers

What would confirm it

At least 40% say they tried the episode's question with their daughter within a week, and 70% would watch the next episode
