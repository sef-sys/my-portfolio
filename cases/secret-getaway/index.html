Research and strategy

Secret Getaway Co.

Service design, UCF, Fall 2025, team project

Problem

Planning a trip takes hours of research and booking. A lot of travelers want the surprise and the adventure without carrying the logistics themselves.

What I did

As founder and CEO on a four person team, I helped build the business model for a mystery travel startup: three packages from a $599 weekend to a $9,999 world tour, three target segments (Lone Voyagers, Perfect Pairs, Thrill Seekers), a competitor review and demand based pricing with a starting price shown up front as an anchor. We then mapped a full service blueprint, from browsing the site to the reveal, against the staff actions and support systems behind it.

Result

A launch ready service plan with measurable standards: a one hour response time on active trips, 90%+ satisfaction and a 75 NPS target, tracked through the CRM and post trip surveys. The slides below are redesigned for this portfolio; the content is from our original deck.
