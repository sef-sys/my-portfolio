Research and strategy

Secret Getaway Co.

Service design, UCF, Nov to Dec 2025

Problem

Planning a trip takes hours of research and booking. A lot of travelers want the surprise and the adventure without carrying the logistics themselves.

What I did

I built a business model for a mystery travel startup and mapped a full service blueprint, from browsing the site to the moment the destination is revealed, against the backstage work of booking vendors and storing customer data in a CRM. I designed three tiers, from a budget getaway to a concierge world tour, for three personas (Lone Voyagers, Perfect Pairs, Thrill Seekers), and set demand based pricing with price anchoring so the unknown destination still feels like good value.

Result

A launch ready model with written service standards: a one hour response time on active trips and a 90%+ post trip satisfaction target, tracked through the CRM and automated surveys.
