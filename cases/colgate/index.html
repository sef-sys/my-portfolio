Colgate consumer behavior audit

Consumer behavior, UCF, Spring 2025, team project

Problem

Toothpaste is a habit purchase. Our team wanted to understand why families keep choosing Colgate Cavity Protection, and where the brand could still win or lose a buyer along the way.

What I did

We audited the product's segmentation, positioning, pricing and promotion: a late majority, lower to middle income family buyer, tiered pricing with Cavity Protection as the most affordable option, and wide distribution through Walmart, CVS and Amazon. Then we mapped the customer journey across five stages, from running out of toothpaste to judging the result, listing the buyer's activities, motivations, questions and barriers at each stage and matching each one to a marketing objective, a channel and a KPI.

Result

An executive summary and a customer journey map showing that trust, price and availability drive the habit, with a recommended action and a measure of success for every stage.
