Research and strategy

Cantopia sales simulation

Sales management simulation, team project

Problem

Our team ran a simulated fragrance company against three rivals over several quarters. Every hiring, pay and territory decision carried into the next quarter.

What I did

We hired about three reps a quarter, raised commissions in steps (2 to 3% early, 4 to 5% later), ran cash incentive contests and let go of reps who underperformed for two quarters in a row. We made real mistakes early, like keeping salary flat too long and buying the territory map late, and fixed them by re ranking reps and testing new incentive structures.

Result

First place in the industry: $36.9M in total revenue and $952K in net income in the simulation, ahead of all three competitors.
