Research and strategy

Coliseum of Comics

Digital marketing strategy, UCF, team project

Problem

A Florida comic book chain with 20+ years in business needed a digital plan to reach younger, budget conscious fans who live on social media, and turn that attention into store visits.

What I did

We started with a quick persona, Jordan, then rebuilt it with research into Alex, a 28 year old collector in Orlando. From there we planned organic posts across awareness, interest, desire and action, a paid Instagram campaign for in store events and a paid TikTok campaign for exclusive drops. We drafted each post and a personalized email with AI first, then rewrote them by hand and noted what the AI got wrong.

Result

An organic, paid and email plan with a $1,000 budget split across social ads, creator collaborations, in store events, email, loyalty and community, mapped to an awareness, convert, retain funnel. The slides below are redesigned for this portfolio; the content is from our original deck.
