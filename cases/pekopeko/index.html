Marketing research, UCF, Jun to Aug 2025

Problem

A croffle startup in Orlando needed to know if local people had heard of it, and how likely they were to try it, before spending more on growth.

What I did

I led outreach to 20+ alumni owned businesses on a 48 hour deadline, then designed and ran a Qualtrics survey with 150 respondents on awareness, discovery channels and how often they buy local food. Beyond descriptive stats, I ran a hypothesis test on likelihood to try and a correlation against how often people already buy from local businesses.

Result

Positioning and awareness recommendations backed by statistical tests, showing whether each pattern was strong enough to act on.
