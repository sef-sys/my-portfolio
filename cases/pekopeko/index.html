Marketing research, UCF, Summer 2025, team project

Problem

A croffle pop up in Orlando, owned by UCF alumni, had just rebranded. It needed to know if local people recognized the brand, understood the product and would try it before spending more on growth.

What I did

I led outreach to 20+ alumni owned businesses on a 48 hour deadline to find the client, then our team designed a Qualtrics survey on awareness, discovery habits and purchase intent. It drew 150 responses, and 103 qualified after screening. Beyond descriptive stats, we ran a hypothesis test on likelihood to try a croffle and a correlation against how often people buy local food.

Result

Interest averaged 8.56 out of 10, well above the level we expected, and buying local food correlated with wanting to try a croffle (r = 0.26, p about 0.008). We recommended a clearer logo, a focus on Instagram and TikTok, gender neutral branding and partnerships with local food events. The slides below are redesigned for this portfolio.
